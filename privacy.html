Privacy Policy

Tone Out: Fire Engine Run · Effective October 10, 2026

Tone Out does not collect any data about you.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting services or advertising.
  • No location access. The town in the game is fictional, and the app never uses your real position.
  • No network connections. The app works fully offline.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone, because none is collected.

What stays on your phone

Your best stars per call and your settings (sound, music, corner map) are saved only on your device so the game remembers them. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Tone Out collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
