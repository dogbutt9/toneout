How to play

  1. Tap Play. The tones drop and the dispatch card shows the address.
  2. Tap Respond. When the bay door is up, hold GAS and steer with the arrow buttons.
  3. Find the address using the grid. Arrive fast and clean for 3 stars.
  4. Finish a call to unlock the next one. Replay any unlocked call from Calls.

Reading an address

  • Animal streets (Antelope, Badger, Coyote…): the hundreds tell you the cross streets. 718 Badger St is between 7th and 8th St.
  • Numbered streets: the hundreds count the animals from the south. 351 3rd St is between Coyote (3rd) and Deer.
  • Odd numbers are on the north and east sides.
  • Mill Creek has bridges only on 2nd, 6th and 10th St.

Common questions

No sound? Check the phone's silent switch and the Sound and Music switches on the title screen.

Want a map? Tap the map button while driving. Free Play shows it by default.

Is this a real town? No. Kettle Falls and every address in it are made up.

Tone Out is a game. Don't use it for real driving or navigation.
